--------------------------------
KEY OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------

1. At 500k records, secondary indexing produced substantial query speedups.

2. smart_197_raw filtering improved from ~3.728 seconds to 0.063 seconds,
   giving approximately 59.2x speedup.

3. smart_5_raw filtering improved from ~3.125 seconds to 0.124 seconds,
   giving approximately 25.2x speedup.

4. Execution plans changed from full table scans over SMART_TELEMETRY to
   index-based range scans over Phoenix secondary index tables.

5. Index creation introduced measurable overhead of approximately 9–10 seconds
   per index, but the read-query improvement was significant for repeated
   analytical queries.

--------------------------------
CONCLUSION
--------------------------------

At 500k records, Apache Phoenix secondary indexes significantly improved
query latency over HBase-backed telemetry data. The results demonstrate that
Phoenix can act as an effective SQL bridge over HBase while still benefiting
from NoSQL-style distributed storage. The tradeoff is additional index build
and maintenance cost, but for repeated analytical queries over SMART telemetry
attributes, the index benefit is substantial.
