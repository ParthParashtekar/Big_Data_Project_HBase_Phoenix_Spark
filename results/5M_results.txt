Key Observations:
1. At 5M records, unindexed queries required approximately 18–21 seconds.
2. Indexed queries completed in under 0.4 seconds.
3. Phoenix execution plans shifted from 6-chunk parallel full scans to index-based range scans.
4. Index build cost increased significantly, requiring about 72–77 seconds per index.
5. The 5M run completed without the region-boundary cache error observed at 2M.

Conclusion:
At 5M records, Apache Phoenix secondary indexing delivered major query latency
improvements over HBase-backed SMART telemetry data. The results demonstrate
that Phoenix can provide SQL access over HBase while achieving substantial
performance gains through secondary indexes. The tradeoff is higher index
creation and maintenance cost, but the benefit is highly favorable for repeated
analytical queries.
